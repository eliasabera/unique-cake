About Us

Where Passion Meets Perfection

Unique Cake House was born from a simple belief—that every celebration deserves a cake as special as the moment itself. Founders Getahun and Kidist turned their passion for baking into a beloved Jimma institution, where every recipe is perfected with love and the finest ingredients.

From birthdays to weddings, graduations to anniversaries, we create edible art that makes your moments unforgettable.
