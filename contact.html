FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about ordering from us

For custom cakes, we recommend placing your order at least 48 hours in advance. For wedding cakes and large orders, please contact us 2–4 weeks ahead. Standard cakes can sometimes be prepared with 24-hour notice.

Yes! We deliver anywhere in Jimma. Orders over ETB 1,500 get free delivery. For orders below that, a small delivery fee applies. Same-day delivery is available for orders placed before 10 AM.

Absolutely! Every cake we make can be fully customized — flavors, colors, themes, toppers, and sizes. Share your idea or reference photo and our designers will bring it to life.

We offer Vanilla, Chocolate, Red Velvet, Carrot, Lemon, Ethiopian Coffee, Strawberry, Marble, and more. Visit our Services page for a full list of flavors, or ask about seasonal specials.

Yes! We offer private tasting sessions, especially recommended for wedding cakes and large events. You can sample flavors, discuss designs, and meet our pastry chef. Book through this page or call us directly.

We accept cash, bank transfer, and mobile payment (Telebirr, CBE Birr). A 50% deposit is required for custom orders, with the balance due on pickup or delivery.
