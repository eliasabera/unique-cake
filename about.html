Our Journey

Where Passion Meets Perfection

Unique Cake House was born from a simple belief—that every celebration deserves a cake as special as the moment itself. Founder Getahun turned his passion for baking into a beloved Jimma institution.

What started as a small kitchen dream has grown into a premier cake destination in Jimma. Every recipe is perfected with love, using the finest ingredients sourced from around the world and locally from Ethiopia's rich agricultural heritage.

From birthdays to weddings, graduations to anniversaries, we create edible art that makes your moments unforgettable. Our commitment to quality, creativity, and customer satisfaction has made us the go-to bakery for celebrations across Jimma and beyond.

— Getahun, Founder

Our Journey

Milestones That Shaped Us

2020

Humble Beginnings

Getahun started baking from his home kitchen, sharing cakes with friends and family.

2021

First Pop-Up Shop

Opened our first pop-up stall at Jimma's weekend market, selling out within hours every Saturday.

2022

Brick & Mortar

Opened our permanent location in Jimma, featuring a full kitchen and display space.

2023

Expansion & Recognition

Named "Best Bakery in Jimma" and expanded our team to meet growing demand.

2024

Wedding Cake Specialist

Became Jimma's premier wedding cake destination, creating over 100 wedding cakes in one year.

2025

Online Presence

Launched our website, bringing Unique Cake House to customers across Ethiopia and beyond.
